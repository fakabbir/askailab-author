**Core Architecture and Models**

- **Transformers:** Neural networks that use self-attention to process words in relation to one another, forming the base of modern language models. [[1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/piyush-ranjan-9297a632_top-gen-ai-terms-you-should-know-in-2025-activity-7379508015143743489-ewu4), [2](https://faculty.ai/en-gb/insights/articles/your-essential-guide-to-genai-terminology-the-top-words-to-know), [3](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1)]
- **Multimodal Models:** Systems that process and integrate multiple data types at once, including text, images, audio, and video. [[1](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1)]
- **Small Language Models (SLMs):** Compact, highly efficient models designed to run quickly on edge devices and local hardware with lower resource use. [[1](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1)]
- **Reasoning Models:** Advanced models engineered to execute multi-step logic and internal deliberation before answering complex math, coding, or logic tasks. [[1](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1)]
- **Mixture of Experts (MoE):** An architecture that routes specific tasks to specialized sub-networks within a larger model, improving speed and efficiency. [[1](https://platform.teamai.com/blog/generative-ai-and-business/ai-terms-everyone-should-know/)]

**Advanced Techniques and Processing**

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** A method that lets an LLM pull fresh facts from external databases before answering, reducing the need to retrain the model. [[1](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/12-essential-genai-terms-you-need-to-know-in-2025-3fd5b928cb37), [2](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1)]
- **Chain-of-Thought (CoT):** A prompting technique that encourages the model to write out its step-by-step reasoning process to solve hard problems. [[1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/piyush-ranjan-9297a632_top-gen-ai-terms-you-should-know-in-2025-activity-7379508015143743489-ewu4)]
- **Embeddings and Vector Databases:** Numerical vectors that capture semantic meaning, stored in specialized vector databases to enable fast similarity searches. [[1](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1), [2](https://platform.teamai.com/blog/generative-ai-and-business/ai-terms-everyone-should-know/)]
- **Quantization:** A process that shrinks model size and memory usage by lowering the precision of its numerical weights.
- **KV Cache:** A memory optimization technique used during inference to store previous key-value calculations and speed up response generation. [[1](https://medium.com/@pankajshakya627/ai-generative-ai-llm-terminology-explained-ca6805a79da5)]

**Systems and Orchestration**

- **Agentic AI:** Autonomous AI systems designed to plan, use tools, and execute multi-step workflows toward a specific goal without constant human prodding.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** An open standard that simplifies how AI models securely connect to external tools, data sources, and software.
- **Guardrails:** Safety and compliance filters placed around an LLM to block toxic language, personal data leaks, and off-topic or illegal responses.
- **LLM-as-a-Judge:** Using a powerful secondary AI model to evaluate, score, and test the outputs of another model automatically. [[1](https://faculty.ai/en-gb/insights/articles/your-essential-guide-to-genai-terminology-the-top-words-to-know), [2](https://medium.com/@chauhanvishal9963/must-know-terms-for-generative-ai-genai-b506e6f25ee1), [3](https://platform.teamai.com/blog/generative-ai-and-business/ai-terms-everyone-should-know/), [4](https://medium.com/@pankajshakya627/ai-generative-ai-llm-terminology-explained-ca6805a79da5)]
