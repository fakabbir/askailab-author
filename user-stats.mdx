**Tripadvisor does not publicly disclose an official, exact Daily Active User (DAU) count**, instead measuring engagement through monthly metrics. [[1](https://resources.review42.com/tripadvisor-statistics/)]

**Key Usage & Traffic Metrics**

- **Monthly Unique Users:** Approximately **400 million to 460 million** people visit the [**<u>Tripadvisor Platform</u>**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/business/about) each month. [[1](https://wiserreview.com/blog/tripadvisor-statistics/)]
- **Monthly Website Visits:** The core Tripadvisor Website averages roughly **74 million to 106 million visits** per month depending on seasonal travel trends. [[1](https://www.semrush.com/website/tripadvisor.com/overview/), [2](https://www.statista.com/topics/3443/tripadvisor/)]
- **Registered Accounts:** The platform holds nearly **490 million registered member accounts**, though most users interact via web search rather than daily app logins. [[1](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/tripadvisor-statistics/), [2](https://getcoai.com/news/tripadvisor-pivots-to-daily-app-as-google-ai-threatens-search-traffic/), [3](https://wiserreview.com/blog/tripadvisor-statistics/)]
- **Engagement Shift:** Tripadvisor has actively worked to pivot its strategy toward increasing daily logged-in mobile app behavior to counter shifts in traditional search traffic. [[1](https://getcoai.com/news/tripadvisor-pivots-to-daily-app-as-google-ai-threatens-search-traffic/)]
